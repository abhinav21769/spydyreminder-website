How to Use Spydy

Everything you need to know about setting up and enjoying your Spydy desktop reminder app.

01 Quick Installation

🍎 For macOS Users

  1. Download SpydyReminder.dmg.
  2. Open the file and drag Spydy to your Applications folder.
  3. If macOS shows a security alert, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

🪟 For Windows Users

  1. Download SpydyReminder-Setup.exe.
  2. Double-click the installer.
  3. Spydy will launch automatically in your Windows System Tray!

02 Setting Reminders & Spider Suits

Click the Spydy icon 🕷️ in your Mac Menu Bar or Windows Taskbar to open the control panel.

  • Custom Message & Presets: Type any message or click chips like 💧 Water, 👁️ Eye Rest, or 🧘 Stretch.
  • Choose Your Suit: Pick between ❤️ Classic, 🖤 Miles Stealth, 💖 Gwen Pink, and 💛 Iron Gold.
  • At Time mode: Schedule alerts at specific clock times (e.g., 08:30 AM).
  • Repeat Every mode: Set interval timers (every 15m, 30m, or 1 hour).
  • Snooze & Dismiss: When Spidey drops down, click ✓ Done or ⏰ Snooze 5m. Press ESC anytime to close.
